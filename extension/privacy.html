Taskbuoy

Privacy policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026 · Extension version 0.2.5

Taskbuoy helps you return to a chosen work intention. Its focus records and optional camera analysis stay on your device. There is no extension account, cloud coaching service, advertising, or analytics service. Playing a motivational video connects to YouTube.

About this policy

This policy describes the Taskbuoy Chrome extension, not the independent privacy practices of Chrome, GitHub, or YouTube.

Browser activity and information you enter

When you choose a work tab or run a focus session, the extension uses Chrome tab/window information, including tab URLs and titles needed for the work-tab picker, tab IDs, active-window state, and idle/locked signals. During a session it uses website domains and timing to classify estimated working, distracting, break, unclassified, and idle/outside-Chrome time.

Local records include your settings, work/distracting domain lists, session intentions, start/end times, domain/category time intervals, reminders, reflections, goals, deadlines, remaining-effort estimates, and the tab reference needed to return to work. Session history does not retain full browsing URL paths or webpage contents. Tab access is not used to download your full Chrome browsing-history database. The extension does not log keystrokes, mouse positions, scrolling, emails, messages, passwords, or payment information.

These records support timers, reminders, optional return/tab closure, local daily summaries, and goal pacing. Active Chrome tabs that are not designated distracting count as estimated work. These estimates do not measure mental attention and do not establish what you are doing in other desktop applications. Free-text intentions and reflections are stored as entered; include only information you want in this browser profile.

Optional camera processing

The camera is off until you explicitly enable it and grant browser camera permission during a focus session. No microphone access is requested. Bundled on-device vision models process camera frames and face landmarks in memory to estimate head/eye direction relative to calibration, face absence, and a possible phone in view.

Camera frames, recordings, face landmarks, and calibration samples are not saved to persistent storage, sent to the developer, or uploaded to an AI service. The extension may retain the latest cue status and a bounded log of reminder reasons and timestamps locally. It does not identify people or create an identity-recognition profile. Camera cues are experimental: a visible phone does not prove scrolling or use, and looking down does not prove distraction.

Stop camera, close its tab, or end the focus session to stop access. During a planned break, analysis pauses while the camera preview remains on. You can also revoke camera permission in Chrome. Browser focus features work without the camera.

Storage, retention, export, and deletion

Settings and records are stored in chrome.storage.local in your Chrome profile. The extension does not use Chrome sync or a developer-hosted database. It retains up to 200 sessions and up to 100 reminder events per session; older entries are removed when those caps are exceeded. Settings and goals remain until you edit or delete them.

Use the dashboard to export your records as a JSON file. After ending an active session, use Delete all records to reset local goals, history, and settings. Uninstalling removes the extension's local storage. Exported files are separate and must be deleted by you. The extension does not add its own encryption to local records; access to your device or Chrome profile can expose them.

External services and sharing

The extension does not send your focus history, domains, goals, reflections, or camera data to the developer, advertisers, or analytics/AI services. There is no sale of extension user data. Bundled camera models do not require a runtime model download.

The optional motivational reset can open after repeated reminders, or when you preview it. It loads an isolated HTTPS player from the Taskbuoy policy-and-player website, which embeds youtube-nocookie.com and offers a YouTube fallback link. The hosted player receives only the selected YouTube video ID, plus ordinary connection information needed to serve it. It does not receive your intentions, activity records, goals, reflections, or camera data. Loading or playing it connects to YouTube and related services, which can receive normal connection/player information such as your IP address and browser details and handle cookies or other identifiers according to their own policies. Your local session records and camera data are not attached to those requests. You can disable motivational resets or avoid the preview. See Google's privacy policy.

If you email support, your email provider transmits the message and sender details you choose to send; these can be used to respond to your request. Do not send session exports or other private information unless you want to share them. The bundled policy page loads no external resources. If you visit an online copy, its hosting provider receives ordinary connection information, such as IP address and browser details, to serve the page.

Permissions and your control

Tabs, idle, alarms, notifications, storage, scripting, offscreen audio, and HTTP/HTTPS website access support the focus-session features described above. Website access displays the on-page coach on user-chosen websites; it is not used to analyze webpage contents. Chrome's restricted pages may not allow the widget. Camera access is requested separately. You control distraction rules, reminder mode, tab closure, sound, video resets, and whether the camera runs.

Limited Use

Taskbuoy uses information received from Chrome APIs only to provide its disclosed focus-session features, in accordance with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. It does not use or transfer this data for advertising, unrelated profiling, creditworthiness, or lending. It does not sell or transfer this data to third parties outside the policy's approved use cases.

Changes and questions

This page will be updated when data practices change. Material changes requiring additional disclosure or consent will be presented in the extension before the new handling begins. For privacy questions or support, use the developer contact information on Taskbuoy's Chrome Web Store listing.
