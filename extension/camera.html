With your permission, local vision models look for a visible face, changes from your calibrated head position, and a phone in view. They cannot know whether you are mentally focused or whether a visible phone is being used.

Camera is off

Start a focus session, then enable the camera here. Nothing is recorded or uploaded.

Keep this camera tab open while working. Camera access ends when you press Stop, close this tab, or the session ends. Closing the preview alone does not imply a loss of focus. Reminders pause during planned breaks. You may need to allow Chrome camera access in macOS Settings.

Calibrate while facing your normal work position. Reading notes, poor lighting, glasses, camera angle, and a phone outside the frame can produce mistakes. A “screen-facing” estimate does not prove eye contact. Raw frames and facial landmarks are discarded after inference; only reminder counts and reasons are kept.
