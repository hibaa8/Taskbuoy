A gentle check-in.

With your permission, local vision models look for sustained downward gaze, a lowered head, leaving the frame, or looking down with a possible phone in view. Normal movement around your calibrated screen position is allowed. They cannot know whether you are mentally focused or whether a visible phone is being used.

CAMERA OFF

Camera is off

Start a focus session, then enable the camera here. Nothing is recorded or uploaded.

Keep this camera tab open while working. Camera access ends when you press Stop, close this tab, or the session ends. Closing the preview alone does not imply a loss of focus. Reminders pause during planned breaks. You may need to allow Chrome camera access in macOS Settings.

During calibration, look naturally around your screen and move a little within your usual work position. Brief glances and tracking dropouts are filtered before the configured reminder delay. Talking is not analyzed, and a visible phone alone does not trigger a reminder. Reading notes, poor lighting, glasses, camera angle, and a phone outside the frame can produce mistakes. A “screen-facing” estimate does not prove eye contact. Raw frames and facial landmarks are discarded after inference; only reminder counts and reasons are kept.
