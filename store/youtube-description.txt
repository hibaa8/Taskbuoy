Taskbuoy | Less Drift. More Done.

DESCRIPTION
One quick scroll. Then another. Sound familiar?

Meet Taskbuoy, a local Chrome extension that helps you get back to what matters. Set your intention, catch distracting tabs, keep your timer in sight, and see where your time goes.

Focus sessions. Visible reminders. Optional automatic return. Goals, reflections, and local activity estimates. Optional experimental camera cues. Your records stay on your device.

A quick look at Taskbuoy: less drift, more done.

Demo records are illustrative. Camera cues and work-time totals do not measure mental attention. Browser tracking covers Chrome, not other desktop apps. Motivational videos connect to the hosted player and YouTube.
