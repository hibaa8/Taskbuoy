SHORT DESCRIPTION
Stay focused with on-page reminders, a live timer, distraction controls, and private daily activity insights.

DETAILED DESCRIPTION
One task. A little less drift.

Taskbuoy helps you stay with the work you meant to do. Start a focus session, choose your work tab, and decide which websites distract you. A small on-page coach keeps your timer visible and helps you return when you wander.

• Catch distracting browsing. Set a grace period and get a visible “Go back to work” reminder when it runs out.
• Choose your level of support. Use gentle reminders or automatically return to your work tab. Optionally close the active distracting tab when you return.
• Keep useful sites available. Unmarked active Chrome tabs count as working, and work-site allowances can override your distraction list.
• Review before you begin. Configure reminders, site rules, sound, and video before the timer starts.
• Keep your rhythm. See your session timer, hear optional chimes, and take planned breaks.
• Understand your day. Review estimated work time, distracting browsing, weekly patterns, and what you actually finished.
• Stay connected to your goals. Add deadlines, update remaining effort, and see simple daily pacing suggestions.
• Get a little encouragement. Optionally open a configurable motivational video after repeated reminders.

OPTIONAL CAMERA COACH
Enable the camera only if you want it. On-device vision models allow natural movement around your calibrated screen position and provide experimental cues for sustained downward gaze, a lowered head, leaving the frame, or looking down with a possible phone in view. Brief glances are filtered. Talking and phone visibility alone do not trigger reminders. These are estimates—not a measure of mental attention, eye contact, or proof of phone scrolling. The core browser features work without the camera.

YOUR DATA, ON YOUR DEVICE
No account or subscription is required. Session records stay in your Chrome profile. Camera frames are processed locally and are not recorded or uploaded. Playing a motivational video connects to the isolated Taskbuoy hosted player and YouTube. Playback depends on YouTube and may require pressing Play.

Built for study sessions, writing, coding, and any task that deserves a little more space. Taskbuoy tracks activity inside Chrome; it does not monitor other desktop apps.
